About TNIRankings

TNIRankings is a yearly ranking of Warhammer 40,000 players who compete in tournaments held in the Republic of Ireland (ROI) and Northern Ireland (NI). Results are sourced from Best Coast Pairings and New Recruit. Each season runs from 1 January to 31 December. The rankings page shows the Northern Ireland (NI) ranking: every player with at least one qualifying NI event that season, including anyone topped up via a force-included override event.

Which events count

An event is included in a season when it is:

  • Located in ROI or NI — determined from the event's location (out-of-region events can be added by override).
  • 12 players or more, and run over 3 rounds or more.
  • 2000-point singles — team/doubles events are excluded unless explicitly opted in.
  • Not online — online tournaments never count.
  • Within the season window (1 Jan – 31 Dec of the year).

Where the points level cannot be determined automatically (e.g. lists were hidden), the event is held for manual review and only counts once confirmed.

How points are awarded

The winner of an event earns the maximum points for that event. Points then decrease linearly to zero across the field:

points(place) = max − (max ÷ N) × (place − 1)

where N is the number of players and place is the final placing (1 = winner). Points never go below zero. The maximum depends on field size and round count:

Example: a 32-player, 5-round event (max 100). The winner scores 100; 2nd = 100 − (100÷32) = 96.875.

A player's season total is the sum of their best 4 counting-event scores — extra events played beyond that don't hurt, but only the top 4 count toward the total. Players are ranked by total points.

Player identity

Players are tracked by their persistent Best Coast Pairings user ID, so a player is followed correctly across events even if their display name, team, or faction changes. Players whose ID cannot yet be resolved from the source data are temporarily excluded; they are picked up as data is refreshed.

Overrides

Three maintained lists let organisers correct edge cases: excluding specific players, force-including events (out-of-region or opted-in team events), and excluding events (e.g. wrong points level). Overrides never bypass the size, round, online, or season-window checks.

A force-included event is exempt from the ROI/NI location check — it can be anywhere — but it only ever tops up players already tracked from a genuine ROI/NI event that season. It never introduces a new player into the rankings on its own. These events are marked with an override badge on the Events page and on a player's event history.

Scoring & windows change over time

The points table and season windows are configuration and may be revised year to year; each season is scored with its own settings.
